System overview
Ticket Sathi aims to improve the experience of users of different theatres in Pokhara valley by
making the current process of booking a movie ticket more user-friendly, secure, convenient and
best of all online. On the landing page users will see the details of the currently running movies
such as their posters, rating (PG, AD, etc), runtime, date and time of showing, etc. When the user
clicks on any movie running in any theatre, the layout / seat arrangement of that cinema hall in
which the movie will be shown inside the theatre will be seen. Seats which are available will be
shown using green color and those which are not will be shown using red color. When a user
selects any seat or multiple seats total price will be shown along with a purchase button. When
the user clicks on the purchase button, verified logged in users will be shown online payment
options while unverified users will be shown sign in and sign up pages and payment options only
after verification.When the verified user chooses any of the payment options and completes the
payment, an electronic ticket will be generated. The electronic ticket will contain an unique
booking ID, movie time and date for showing seat number and a QRcode. Upon the designated
time, a staff member from the theatre will verify the ticket using a QR scanner. The staff will
reject invalid tickets and accept users with valid tickets. Website admin will handle movie
scheduling , seat management and ticket cancellations.
